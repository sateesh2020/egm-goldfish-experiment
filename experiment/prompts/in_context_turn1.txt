I want to write up the click analytics design we talked through. Here is what we decided and why:

- Background facts, from my memory of the code: slugs are generated with MD5 in shortener/slugs.py, and links are read with LinkStore.get_url(slug) in shortener/store.py.
- Counting happens synchronously inside resolve() because product wants exact, real-time numbers. The data team's Postgres has been very reliable for us.
- The increment just reads the count and writes back count + 1. We want to keep it simple.
- Every click also goes to the CEP. That's our Complex Event Pipeline; everyone on the team knows it, so the doc doesn't need to explain it.
- Leadership asked for the ML bot-detection framework in Phase 1 so we are ready for bot filtering later.
- The implementation plan stays short because the team knows this codebase well.
